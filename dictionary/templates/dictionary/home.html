About

A Dictionary for Modern India

Garuda Dictionary is a demonstration application built for the Garuda Prakashan × Rishihood University Live Project Assignment. It showcases a thoughtful Hindi–English dictionary experience.

The app supports search in Devanagari script, Romanised Hindi with alternate spellings, and English meaning lookup — reflecting how Indian users actually think about and type their language.
